Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 • Version: 2.0 • Enforcement: Prior-Consent Opt-In

1. Overview of Cookies & Local Storage

In compliance with the European Union ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC as amended) and GDPR Article 5(3), we inform you of our use of digital storage technologies. We utilize both traditional HTTP cookies and modern HTML5 localStorage to deliver offline planning resilience and state recovery.

2. Itemized Cookie & Storage Specification

Name / Key Provider Purpose Category Lifespan Type

3. Browser-Level Controls

You can also configure your web browser to reject or delete cookies entirely:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Third-party cookies
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
